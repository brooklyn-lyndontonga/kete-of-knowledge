# Kete of Knowledge — how to install the test app

Kia ora, and thank you for helping test this app.

This is an early version, so some things will be unfinished and some pages may say "sample" or look empty. **That's expected.** If something doesn't work or doesn't make sense, that's exactly what we want to hear about — you're not breaking it, you're helping.

Find your phone type below and follow those steps.

---

# If you have an iPhone

**You'll need to do this once. It takes about five minutes.**

### Step 1 — Install TestFlight

TestFlight is a free app made by Apple. It's how Apple lets people try apps before they're finished. You can't install our app without it.

1. Open the **App Store** on your phone
2. Search for **TestFlight**
3. Tap **Get** to install it (it's free)

You don't need to open TestFlight yet.

### Step 2 — Find your invitation email

Check your email for a message from Apple with **Kete of Knowledge** in it.

**If you can't find it:**
- Check your junk or spam folder
- Make sure you're looking in the right email account — it will have gone to the email attached to your Apple ID, which might be an older address than the one you use every day
- Let us know and we'll send it again

### Step 3 — Accept and install

1. Open the email and tap **View in TestFlight**
2. TestFlight will open
3. Tap **Accept**, then **Install**

Kete of Knowledge will appear on your home screen like any other app.

### Step 4 — Open it and have a look around

That's you done. Use it however feels natural.

---

# If you have an Android phone

**This takes about two minutes.**

### Step 1 — Open the link we sent you

You'll have received a link by email or text. Tap it on your phone (not on a computer — it needs to be the phone you'll use).

### Step 2 — Tap Install

Follow the prompts.

**Your phone may show a warning** saying something like "this type of file can harm your device" or asking permission to install from an unknown source.

This is normal for apps that aren't in the Play Store yet. Ours isn't there yet because it's still being tested. Tap through to allow it and carry on.

If you'd rather not, that's completely fine — just tell us and we'll sort another way.

### Step 3 — Open it and have a look around

Kete of Knowledge will be on your home screen.

---

# Using the app

**You don't need an account to look around.** Tap "continue as guest" and you can read everything.

**To save anything** — symptoms, medicines, goals, contacts — you'll need to sign in. There's no password. You enter your email, we send you a link, you tap it, and you're in. The link only lasts 15 minutes, so tap it soon after it arrives.

---

# What we'd love you to try

No need to do all of these. Whatever you have time for.

- Have a look around and see what you find
- Read about a heart condition in the library
- Add a symptom, as if you were recording how you felt today
- Add one of your medicines
- Add a contact — someone in your whānau
- Set a reminder and see if it goes off
- Try switching the app into te reo Māori (in Settings)
- Try using it somewhere with no signal, and see if it still works

---

# What we'd love to know

- Was there anything you couldn't find or work out?
- Was there anything confusing, or any words that didn't make sense?
- Did anything go wrong — the app closing, something not saving?
- If you used it in te reo Māori, did anything read oddly?
- Would you use this yourself? Would you show it to your whānau?

**Please be honest.** If something annoyed you or you gave up on it, that's the most useful thing you can tell us. We'd much rather hear it now than after it's finished.

---

# A note about your information

Anything you record in the app is stored on your own phone. If you sign in, it's also backed up to your account so you don't lose it.

We can't see what you write. If you'd rather not put real health information in during testing, made-up information works just as well.

You can delete everything at any time — it's in Settings.
