# Te reo Māori review sheet — Kete of Knowledge

**For:** a fluent te reo Māori speaker
**Time needed:** roughly 1–2 hours
**Returned to:** Brooklyn Lyndon-Tonga

---

## What this is

Every piece of text that appears in the Kete of Knowledge app, in English and te reo Māori.

The te reo was assembled by reusing the wording already present in the original app designs, with the remainder written by an AI assistant. **None of it has been checked by a speaker.** That is what this review is for.

## What we need from you

Read the te reo column. Where it is wrong, awkward, or not what you would say to a kaumātua, write the correct wording in the last column. If a line is fine, leave it blank.

Please pay particular attention to:

- **Macrons.** These carry meaning and there are known inconsistencies.
- **Possessives.** `taku` vs `tāku` vs `tōku` are used inconsistently and may be wrong for body parts.
- **Health language.** Some of this describes symptoms and medicines. Plain, warm and respectful matters more than literal accuracy.
- **Tone.** This app is for whānau managing heart conditions, including older people. Clinical or cold phrasing is a problem even if the words are correct.

You do not need to touch any code. This document is the whole job.
