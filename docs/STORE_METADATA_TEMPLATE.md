## App Name
Kete of Knowledge

## Short Description
Empowering whānau with accessible health, medication, and wellbeing information in one kete.

## Full Description
Kete of Knowledge is a health and wellbeing companion designed to support whānau in managing their health journey.
Key features include:
- **Condition Library**: Trustworthy information on common health conditions and symptoms.
- **Medicines Hub**: A personal space to track medications and access simplified drug information.
- **Whakataukī of the Day**: Daily Māori proverbs to uplift and inspire.
- **Profile & Settings**: Customisable profiles to keep your health data secure and personalised.
- **Resources**: Direct access to external health services and support networks.

Built with simplicity and culture at its core, Kete of Knowledge brings essential health tools together in one easy-to-use basket.
