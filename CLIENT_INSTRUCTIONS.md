# Kete of Knowledge – Application Testing Guide

Kia ora! Thank you for helping us test the **Kete of Knowledge** app.

Please follow the instructions below to install and test the application on your device.

---

## 📱 1. Installation

### Option A: Android (Samsung, Pixel, etc.)
**This is the best experience.**
1.  **Download** the app file (`.apk`) from the link executed.
2.  **Open** the file on your phone.
3.  If asked, allow installation from **"Unknown Sources"** (this is required for test apps not yet on the Play Store).
4.  Tap **Install**.

### Option B: iPhone (iOS) - Native App
*Note: Apple prevents installing test apps directly without a paid account. You must use Expo Go.*
1.  Download **Expo Go** from the Apple App Store.
2.  **Scan the QR Code** provided by the developer.

### Option C: Web Browser (No App Install)
*Works on iPhone, Android, and Desktop.*
1.  The developer will send you a **website link**.
2.  Open it in Chrome or Safari.
3.  The app will run inside your browser.

---

## 🔐 2. How to Login
1.  Open the App.
2.  Enter your **email address** on the login screen.
3.  Tap **"Send Magic Link"**.
4.  Go to your email inbox (on your phone).
5.  Open the email from "Kete of Knowledge" and tap the **login link**.
6.  You will be redirected back to the app, logged in!

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes
*   **Server Availability**: This test version runs on a development server. Please coordinate with the developer to ensure the system is **ONLINE** before testing.
*   **Data**: Any data you create (profiles, notes) is saved to the test database.
*   **Feedback**: If you encounter any issues or "Red Screens" (errors), please take a screenshot and send it to us.
