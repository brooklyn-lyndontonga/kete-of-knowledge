# Client Requirements Checklist

To successfully launch and maintain the **Kete of Knowledge** app, we need the following items from you. These ensure that you own your intellectual property and have full control over the app's future.

## 1. App Store Accounts (Critical for Release)
You need to enroll in the developer programs to publish apps under your organization's name.

### 🍎 Apple Developer Program
*   **Purpose**: To publish the iOS app to the App Store.
*   **Cost**: ~$99 USD / year.
*   **Action**:
    1.  Enroll at [developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/).
    2.  You will likely need a **D-U-N-S Number** (a unique business identifier).
    3.  Once enrolled, invite me as an **Admin** or **App Manager** to your team.
    4.  **Email to invite**: [YOUR_EMAIL]

### 🤖 Google Play Console
*   **Purpose**: To publish the Android app to the Google Play Store.
*   **Cost**: $25 USD (one-time fee).
*   **Action**:
    1.  Sign up at [play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console/signup).
    2.  Once verifiable, invite me as a **User** with "Admin" permissions.
    3.  **Email to invite**: [YOUR_EMAIL]

## 2. Hosting & Infrastructure
The app's "brain" (backend server and database) needs a home.

### Railway (Recommended)
*   **Purpose**: Hosts the Server, Database, and Admin Panel.
*   **Action**:
    1.  Create an account at [railway.app](https://railway.app).
    2.  Add a payment method (for monthly hosting costs).
    3.  Generate an **Invite Link** or invite me to your team so I can transfer the project to you.

## 3. Legal & Compliance
App stores require these links to be visible to the public.

*   [ ] **Privacy Policy URL**: A webpage explaining how data is handled.

*   [ ] **Support URL**: A contact page or email address where users can get help.
