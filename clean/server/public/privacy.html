Privacy Policy

Kete of Knowledge — Last updated: [DATE]

Kete of Knowledge ("we", "our", "us") is a health and wellbeing information app for whānau, provided by [ORGANISATION NAME]. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Your information on your device

Everything you record in the app — your profile, goals, symptoms, medicines, notes, checklists, reflections and contacts — is stored locally on your device.

If you use the app as a guest, this information never leaves your phone. We do not collect any personal information from guests.

You can export a copy of this information, or permanently delete it from your device, at any time from Settings → My data.

Information we collect if you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in:

Email address — used only to send you a secure sign-in link. We do not use it for marketing and we do not share it.

Backup of your health information — the information you record (profile, goals, symptoms, medicines, notes, reminders, checklists, reflections and contacts) is backed up to your account on our server so it can be restored if you change or lose your device. It is linked to your account, transmitted over an encrypted connection, and accessible only through your sign-in.

You can delete your backed-up information from our server at any time from Settings → My data → Delete my data, which removes it from both your device and our systems.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, photos, or advertising identifiers. The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics, and we do not sell or share your information.

Content in the app

Health information shown in the app (conditions, learning resources, whakataukī) is general educational content delivered from our server. It is not medical advice and does not replace advice from your doctor or health provider. If you feel unwell, contact them or call 111.

Data security

Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and can only be used once. Your email address is held in a secure database with access restricted to authorised administrators.

Your choices

You can use the app as a guest without providing any personal information; export or delete your on-device information from Settings at any time; and ask us to delete your email address by contacting brooklynlt24@gmail.com.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be shown in the app's consent screen, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
